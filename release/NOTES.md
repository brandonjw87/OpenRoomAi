**Same room. Same stuff. Way more open floor.**

Open Area AI maps a room with your phone, measures every square foot of usable open floor and every walkway's real width, then tells you exactly what to move, often just a few inches.

## Which file?
| File | For |
|---|---|
| **OpenAreaAI-v2.0-pixel-arm64.apk** (8.2 MB) | Pixel and nearly every modern phone (64-bit ARM) |
| **OpenAreaAI-v2.0-universal.apk** (14.8 MB) | Anything else on Android 8.0+, including 32-bit ARM phones and x86_64 Chromebooks |

## Install
1. Open this page on your phone and tap the APK.
2. If Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps.
3. Open **Open Area AI**. AR scanning uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore); *Sketch by size* and the demo room work on any phone.

## What's new in 2.0
- **⚡ Micro-Nudges (the 3-inch optimizer):** small, realistic moves ranked by square feet and walkway inches gained, e.g. "Push the Sofa flush against Wall C (9½″) · +4.8 sq ft open floor". Preview, Apply, Undo.
- **⬜ Open zone & flow:** your biggest open rectangle with its exact size, usable open floor, and every walkway's real width (44″+ wide · 36–44″ clear · 30–36″ tight · under 30″), with pinch points labeled.
- **One-tap goals:** Max open center · Widest flow paths · Quick wins, with a Before → After card and a before/after wipe.
- Inches & feet or cm & m everywhere, undo/redo, L-shaped rooms, smarter move plans.

Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/brandonjw87/OpenRoomAi#readme).
